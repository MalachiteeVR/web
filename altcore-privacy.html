Privacy Policy

Altcore is designed around data minimization: collect what is needed to operate the service, protect users, and keep the platform working, rather than building an advertising profile around you.

Last updated: October 29, 2026
PRIVACY PRINCIPLE: Altcore is not designed to sell your personal information or use private conversations as an advertising profile.

1. Scope

This Privacy Policy describes how Altcore and services operated as part of the Altcore project are intended to handle information when you use the platform.

Altcore is an 18+ service. You must be at least 18 years old to use the platform. This policy does not replace the Altcore Terms of Service.

2. Information We Collect

Altcore aims to minimize collection. The exact information processed can vary depending on which features you use and how the service is deployed.

CategoryExamplesWhy it may be needed
Account informationUsername, account identifier, authentication information, profile settingsCreating and operating your account and keeping it secure

We do not intend to collect unrelated personal information merely because it might be useful for profiling or advertising.

3. How We Use Information

Information may be processed for the following purposes:

  • Providing accounts, messaging, communities, voice/video, file sharing, and other features you request.
  • Authenticating users and protecting accounts.
  • Preventing spam, fraud, abuse, malicious activity, and unauthorized access.
  • Diagnosing outages, crashes, performance problems, and other technical issues.
  • Maintaining and improving reliability, compatibility, and security.
  • Enforcing the Terms of Service and responding to legitimate reports.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

Altcore is not intended to build an advertising profile from your private conversations or sell your personal information to advertisers.

4. Messages, Files, and User Content

When you send a message, upload a file, or otherwise submit content, the service has to process and store enough information to deliver that content to its intended recipients and provide the features you use.

Your content remains yours. Altcore does not claim ownership of your messages, artwork, files, or other original content simply because you use the service.

Content may be accessed or processed by authorized systems or personnel when reasonably necessary for service operation, security, abuse prevention, moderation, support, legal compliance, or troubleshooting. Access should be limited to what is reasonably necessary for the relevant purpose.

Altcore should not be interpreted as providing end-to-end encryption unless a particular feature explicitly states that it does. Server-side storage and transport security are different from end-to-end encryption.

5. When Information May Be Shared

Altcore does not intend to sell personal information to advertisers. Information may nevertheless be disclosed in limited circumstances, including:

  • At your direction: when you intentionally publish content, join a community, send a message, or otherwise choose to make information available to others.
  • Service providers: where outside providers are necessary for infrastructure, hosting, storage, email, payments, security, or other service functions, subject to the applicable service arrangement.
  • Legal requirements: when disclosure is required by valid legal process or applicable law.
  • Safety and security: when reasonably necessary to investigate serious abuse, fraud, security incidents, threats, or activity that could cause substantial harm.
  • Business changes: if Altcore or relevant service assets are reorganized, transferred, or acquired, information may be involved in that transaction subject to applicable law and any required notices.

6. Retention and Deletion

Altcore's intended approach is to retain information for no longer than reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, subject to legitimate operational, security, legal, and backup requirements.

Retention periods can differ by data type. For example, account records may need to exist while an account is active, while security logs may be retained for a limited period to investigate abuse or incidents.

Deleting an account or content may not instantly remove every copy from backups, caches, logs, or systems required for security and legal compliance. Those copies should be removed or overwritten according to the applicable retention process.

Where the service provides account or content deletion controls, those controls are the primary way to request deletion. Additional privacy requests can be made through the official Altcore contact channel.

7. Security

Altcore is intended to use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards appropriate to the service, including access controls, authentication protections, secure transport, monitoring, and measures intended to prevent unauthorized access.

No internet service can guarantee absolute security. If you believe an account or security system has been compromised, report it through the official Altcore support or security channel as soon as practical.

8. Your Choices and Privacy Requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information. These may include rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing.

Altcore may also provide account controls that let you edit profile information, manage sessions, delete content, or close your account.

Requests may require reasonable verification so that one person cannot obtain or delete another person's information.

9. Third-Party Services

Altcore may interact with third-party services for infrastructure or optional features. Those services may have their own privacy policies and terms.

Third-party links, integrations, or community services are not automatically controlled by Altcore. Review the applicable third party's privacy practices before providing it with information.

10. Age Restriction

Altcore is an adults-only platform and is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly permit minors to create or maintain accounts.

If we become aware that an account belongs to someone under 18, we may take appropriate action, including restricting or terminating the account and deleting associated information where appropriate.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Altcore's architecture, features, legal requirements, or data practices change.

When changes materially affect how personal information is handled, we will make a reasonable effort to provide prominent notice through the service or another appropriate channel.

12. Contact

For privacy questions, data requests, security concerns, or questions about this policy, use the official Altcore support or privacy contact channel provided by the service.
