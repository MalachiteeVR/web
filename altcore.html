MALACHITEVR · ALTCORE

Social communication without the bloat.

Altcore is an 18+ social platform inspired by the useful parts of Discord, built with a different philosophy: keep the communication tools people actually use, respect privacy, and don't turn basic functionality into a maze of subscriptions.

Open Altcore Privacy Policy Terms of Service

Privacy first

Altcore is designed around data minimization rather than building an advertising profile around your conversations and activity.

No feature paywall

The goal is a useful communication platform without locking ordinary core features behind increasingly expensive subscription tiers.

18+ platform

Altcore is intended only for adults 18 and older. The age restriction does not override the platform's content and safety rules.

Communities & DMs

Chat with friends, participate in communities, and keep conversations organized without unnecessary platform clutter.

Open-minded design

Altcore aims to give communities useful controls while keeping platform-wide rules focused on actual abuse, safety, security, and legal requirements.

Built by MalachiteVR

Altcore is part of the MalachiteVR ecosystem and is being developed as an independent, privacy-focused social platform.
